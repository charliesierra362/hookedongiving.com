For Spouses & Dates

A Day of Your Own

While your partner is on the river, Casper is yours to enjoy. We've curated a day that blends history, indulgence, and Wyoming charm — pace it however you like.

☕

Morning · 8:00 – 10:30 AM

Slow Start, Sweet Beginnings

Start with coffee at CitiWerks Cafe — an upscale downtown coffeehouse attached to the event space, serving pour-overs, fresh-baked baguettes, and a small but excellent breakfast menu on real china. For something heartier, Sherrie's Place is a beloved local diner where the cinnamon rolls are legend and every regular is greeted by name. Or linger at Cottage Cafe, tucked inside a converted historic home — house-made pastries and unhurried service.

◆ CitiWerks Cafe · 162 S Wolcott St
◆ Sherrie's Place · 310 W Yellowstone Hwy
◆ Cottage Cafe · 116 S Lincoln St
🏛️

Late Morning · 10:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Where the Trails Converged

Casper is the only point in the nation where the Oregon, California, Mormon, and Pony Express Trails crossed paths. The National Historic Trails Interpretive Center brings that history to life with award-winning films and an immersive covered-wagon river-crossing simulator. Free admission, about 90 minutes to explore — and it's just up Poplar Street from the host hotel.

Prefer something quieter? The Nicolaysen Art Museum ("The Nic") on Collins Drive features rotating contemporary exhibits with a distinctly Wyoming sensibility.

◆ Interpretive Center · 1501 N Poplar St
🥂

Lunch · 12:30 – 2:00 PM

Lunch in Downtown Casper

Head downtown for lunch and a stroll. Racca's Pizzeria Napoletana serves authentic coal-fired pies; The Branding Iron plates Wyoming-raised Black Angus burgers (their Kona Burger took Travel Channel's Best in Wyoming in 2019); or for something upscale and elegant, The Refinery at Three Crowns overlooks the golf course with a spectacular view.

💆
